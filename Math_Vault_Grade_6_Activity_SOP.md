## 1. Executive Summary & Core Objective
**Math Vault** is an interactive, gamified 3D classroom activity engineered to replace traditional, repetitive math drills with a high-stakes, collaborative "Vault Heist" escape room challenge.

* **Primary Learning Outcome**: Rapid development of mental calculation fluency, working memory capacity, number sense intuition, and algorithmic agility in 6th graders.
* **The Zero Pen & Paper Rule**: Every problem is strictly calculated mentally. Problems are engineered with benchmark factors, complementary properties, and clean integer relationships so students hold intermediate values in their cognitive working memory.

---

## 2. Technical & Activity Architecture
The activity layout contains 4 dedicated zones:
1. **Dedicated Static Question Terminal**: Mounted directly above the 3D Vault. Features giant, high-contrast typography (`(-5) × (-9) = ?`) with black borders, topic badges, and a collapsible student hint toggle. Decoupled from all moving parts so it never moves or disappears.
2. **3D Progressive Vault Door**: A circular industrial vault door with a central 6-spoke golden master wheel. Correct answers trigger a 180° mechanical gear rotation and swing the heavy door open to reveal gold bars, rubies, and diamonds inside.
3. **Dual Squad Interactive Consoles**: Rapid digit input, streak multipliers, key slots (1–3 keys), and 3-strike alarm triggers.
4. **HUD Shoulder Telemetry**: Left shoulder displays Round Counter (`ROUND 1/5`), right shoulder displays animated Time Left timer (`⏱ 00:20`).

## 3. Curriculum Mapping (Specifically for Grade 6)
Grade 6 is the critical bridge from concrete arithmetic to abstract algebra and signed operations. Math Vault maps directly to CBSE Grade 6 NCERT, ICSE, and Cambridge Stage 7:

| Grade 6 Mathematical Domain | Mental Math Mechanism | Sample In-Game Code |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Integers & Signed Arithmetic** | Applying integer sign rules mentally: `(-) × (-) = (+)`, `(-) × (+) = (-)`. | `(-5) × (-9) = 45`<br>`(-15) + (-28) = -43` |
| **BODMAS / Order of Operations** | Mental operational precedence (Brackets -> Multiplication -> Addition). | `20 - 4 × 3 = 8`<br>`5 × (12 - 4) = 40` |
| **Ratios, Fractions & Percentages** | Benchmark percentage conversions (25%, 50%, 10%), HCF simplifications. | `25% of 80 = 20`<br>`Simplify 18:24 = 3:4` |
| **Number Patterns & Sequences** | Detecting incremental jumps, doubling patterns, and square series. | `3, 7, 15, 31, [ ? ] = 63`<br>`5, 11, 17, 23, [ ? ] = 29` |
| **Mental Geometry & Angles** | Complementary (90°), supplementary (180°), and triangle angle sums (180°). | `Complement of 35° = 55°`<br>`Third angle: 60°, 70°, [ ? ] = 50°` |

## 4. Bloom's Taxonomy Pedagogy Framework
Math Vault engages multiple cognitive levels across Bloom's Revised Taxonomy:

* **Level 1: Remembering (Knowledge Recall)**: Rapidly recalling multiplication tables up to 15×15, perfect squares, and integer sign rules without reference sheets.
* **Level 2: Understanding (Conceptualization)**: Recognizing mathematical relationships, such as realizing that 25% of a number is equivalent to dividing by 4.
* **Level 3: Applying (Mental Execution)**: Executing multi-step BODMAS operations mentally and carrying numbers within memory buffers.
* **Level 4: Analyzing (Deconstruction)**: Identifying sequence patterns and working backwards with inverse operations.
* **Level 5: Evaluating & Strategy (Decision Making)**: Weighing risk vs. reward—submitting quickly for a streak bonus vs. taking 3 seconds to mentally verify to prevent a 3-strike team lockout.

---

## 5. Cognitive Science: How Learning Occurs
1. **Math Anxiety Reduction**: Transforming test pressure into an exciting vault heist frees working memory and increases student engagement.
2. **Immediate Dopamine Reinforcement**: The mechanical vault wheel rotation, unlocking sounds, and treasure reveals build positive neural associations with mathematics.
3. **Working Memory Capacity Expansion**: Forcing students to compute mentally without scratchpads exercises executive cognitive functions.
4. **Peer Strategy & Cooperative Learning**: Squad discussions promote active peer-to-peer explanation of mental arithmetic shortcuts.

---

## 6. Facilitator Workflow & Rules of Engagement
1. **Setup (3 Mins)**: Select Grade 6, choose Bloom's Topic, set Heist Rounds (5, 10, or 20), enter Team Names.
2. **Briefing (2 Mins)**: Reiterate the strict Zero Pen & Paper Rule and explain the 3-Strike Lockout mechanic.
3. **Execution (20–30 Mins)**: Teams race against the 20-second timer to submit codes. Wrong answers create a "Steal Opportunity" for the opposing team.
4. **Debrief (5 Mins)**: Celebrate the treasury breach and conduct a 3-minute discussion on the mental strategies utilized.

---

## 7. Assessment & Evaluation Rubric
* **Mental Speed & Fluency**: Rated on response time (<7s for Master level, 8–14s for Proficient).
* **Sign Rule Automaticity**: Instant handling of negative integers and mixed operators.
* **BODMAS Operational Priority**: Precision in solving multi-term expressions mentally.
* **Teamwork & Risk Control**: Strategic management of strikes and steal opportunities.
